The included `pine.glb`, `oak.glb`, `birch.glb`, `shrub.glb`, `rock.glb`, `reeds.glb` and `flowers.glb` are newly generated original low-poly geometry using `scripts/generate_assets.py`. They contain no third-party downloaded meshes, photographs, textures or logos.
