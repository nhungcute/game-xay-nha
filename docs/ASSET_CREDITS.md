# Asset Credits (D030 — trees & bushes)

All environment trees and bushes were replaced in D030 with imports from the owner-supplied pack in
`map/tree/` (not committed), processed by `scripts/import-trees.mjs` into game-ready GLBs
(`apps/web/public/assets/models/env/` and `models/env-by-sa/`). Parameters and per-model measurements:
`scripts/trees.config.json`.

## Modifications ("đã chỉnh sửa")

- **Deciduous (Stylized Tree, CC-BY-4.0):** node transforms baked and units converted to meters; leaf
  alpha cutoff lowered 0.865 → 0.4 (the source value clipped the canopy to bare branches); leaves and bark
  packed into one 512×640 atlas (leaf slot ≤ 512 px, flat bark colour slot 128 px); 8112-triangle bark
  simplified with meshoptimizer; the 2997 leaf cards thinned to a seeded random subset (~19 %) enlarged to
  compensate area; four variants generated by hue-rotating the canopy to greens (01–03) while 04 keeps the
  original red-orange; per-variant height 4.2–5.6 m with canopy clamped ≤ 3.2 m; plus the far-LOD billboards.
- **Conifer (Fur Tree, CC-BY-SA-4.0):** `KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness` converted to metal-rough
  (diffuse → baseColor, metalness 0, roughness 1 − glossiness); the trunk section buried ~0.5 m below the
  ground plane removed (y = 0 stays the ground); textures downsampled into one 512×640 atlas and brightened
  15–25 % per variant (the source is dark); branch cards simplified 2240 → ~1408 triangles; four
  height/taper/colour variants (4.5–6.0 m, canopy ≤ 2.6 m); plus the far-LOD billboards.
  **The modified conifer models are redistributed under CC-BY-SA 4.0** (same license, no additional
  restrictions) — they live in `models/env-by-sa/` beside their own `LICENSE.md`, and the in-game
  "Ghi công" panel labels them CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- **Bush (Small bush, CC-BY-4.0):** alpha mode BLEND → MASK (cutoff 0.4), `KHR_materials_specular` removed;
  4096² texture downsampled to a 512² atlas and brightened (the sprig scan is very dark); the source model is
  a botanical sprig on a long stem — its 16 sprig components are rearranged into a dome so it reads as a
  shrub at game scale; four hue/scale variants (0.9–1.1 m); material marked unlit (`KHR_materials_unlit`) so
  the scan shading does not blacken under the game lights; plus the far-LOD billboards.

author, license (linked to its text), source link and the "đã chỉnh sửa" notice — read live from
`assets/manifest.json`.
